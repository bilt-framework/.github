# Bilt

**An open-source framework for scientific and engineering modelling.**

Bilt makes it easier to build, connect, solve and interrogate complex models. Rather than prescribing how a calculation should be performed, Bilt lets you describe the relationships between physical quantities and works out how to solve for the information you need.

### Core capabilities

- **Acausal modelling** — solve models in different directions without rewriting equations.
- **Systems of systems** — build complex models from smaller, reusable components.
- **Dimensional analysis** — work with physical quantities and units.
- **Coupled systems** — identify and solve interdependent equations.
- **Explainability** — understand how results were calculated and which assumptions were used.
- **AI-ready modelling** — provide explicit, structured and machine-readable representations of scientific knowledge.

### The Bilt ecosystem

This organisation brings together the Bilt framework, supporting libraries, example models and development tools.

Our aim is to make scientific and engineering models easier to develop, share, reuse and trust.

### Get involved

Bilt is under active development. Contributions, feedback and discussions are welcome, whether you are a scientist, engineer or software developer.
